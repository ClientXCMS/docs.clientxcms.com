# v2.12.0 - Security Update**

We are pleased to announce the release of version **v2.12.0** of ClientXCMS, which brings numerous improvements and new features to enhance your user experience.
![version v2.14.0](/img/blog/versions/v2.12.0.png)

<!-- truncate -->

- `➕` Added 2FA for Clients/Administrators: Secure your accounts further with two-factor authentication.
- `➕` History of modifications/actions performed by the system: Keep track of modifications and actions performed for better transparency.
- `➕` Added role and permission system: Manage access and permissions in a finer and more secure way.
- `➕` Virtualizor Extension: With reseller automation,
- `➕` Mass actions: Save time with mass actions on your services, invoices, and IPAM.
- `➕` Maintenance system: Easily set up maintenance periods for your client area.
- `➕` Added security captcha: Strengthen security with captchas to protect your client area.
- `➕` Ability to offer subdomains for purchase: Offer more flexibility to your clients by allowing them to put their site on one of your domains.

Fixes:

- `🛠️` Fixed various bugs to improve system stability and performance.
