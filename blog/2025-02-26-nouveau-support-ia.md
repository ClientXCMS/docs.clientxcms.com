# 🚀 New AI Support to Help You!

We have implemented artificial intelligence to assist you on our Discord server and on the CLIENTXCMS website. This AI, named **Antoine**, is designed to answer your questions and solve your common problems.
![Antoine AI](/img/blog/nouveau-support-ia/image.png)
<!-- truncate -->
## How to Use Antoine AI?
To interact with Antoine AI, simply ask your questions directly on the CLIENTXCMS Discord server. It is available in the **「🤖」question-antoine** channel or via private message.
## 🤖 24/7 Support
Antoine AI offers fast assistance available at all times. Admittedly, it is still limited, but it can already answer many questions and solve your errors effectively.

## 🎯 Why This AI?
More than 60% of support requests concern errors that users can solve themselves. Antoine thus helps relieve the volunteer team so they can focus on interventions requiring human expertise.

## 📌 Beta Version: Your Help is Valuable!
Antoine AI is in the testing phase, and we are counting on your feedback to improve it. Eventually, it could even handle questions related to your licenses! But for this, we need your participation to enrich its knowledge base.

📩 Have a question or suggestion? Contact @FreezMod directly on Discord

Thank you for your help and feedback!
