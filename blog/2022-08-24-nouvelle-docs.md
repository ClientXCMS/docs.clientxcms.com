Hello everyone!

We are pleased to present the official **CLIENTXCMS** documentation in open-source. It has been designed to guide you in understanding, installing, and exploring all the features of our CMS. We are convinced that there is still much to discover, even for the most experienced users!

<!-- truncate -->

The documentation is constantly evolving. Every week, we add new pages, sections, and details to make it even more complete. Pages on payment methods are already available, and gradually, we will also detail how delivery modules such as **Pterodactyl**, **Proxmox**, **Plesk**, and many others work.

If you have suggestions, if you spot mistakes or improvements to make, don't hesitate to contact us via our [technical support](https://clientxcms.com/client/support). Due to my dyslexia, some errors may persist.

You can also contribute to enriching the documentation by submitting [pull requests](https://github.com/ClientXCMS/clientxcms-docs/pulls) on our GitHub repository. After verification, we will integrate your additions into the official documentation.

Happy reading and see you soon on [clientxcms.com](https://clientxcms.com)!
