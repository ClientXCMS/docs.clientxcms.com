# v2.10.0 - Personalization Update**

We are pleased to announce the release of version **v2.10.0** of ClientXCMS, which brings numerous improvements and new features to enhance your user experience.
![version v2.14.0](/img/blog/versions/v2.10.0.png)

<!-- truncate -->

- `➕` **Customizable menus at top and bottom of page**: Enjoy greater flexibility in navigation by adding custom menus at the top and bottom of your pages.
- `➕` **Product, configuration, and group cloning**: Simplify your creation process by quickly cloning existing products with all their configurations.
- `➕` **Email sending at different times before expiration**: Stay in touch with your users by sending reminders at different times before service expiration, with predefined and customizable options.
- `➕` **Host earnings page**: Easily visualize your earnings as a host with a new dedicated page.
