# New ClientXCMS Forum
We are pleased to announce the launch of the **new ClientXCMS forum**! This forum has been designed to improve our community's experience, facilitate exchanges, and offer a friendly space to discuss everything related to ClientXCMS.
![New ClientXCMS forum](/img/blog/nouveau-forum/image.png)
<!-- truncate -->
## Why a New Forum?
In line with the open-sourcing of ClientXCMS, we wanted to create a forum that reflects the values of transparency and collaboration. The new forum is based on [Flarium](https://flarium.org/), an open source CMS for forums, which allows us to better meet our community's needs.

## Where to Find the Forum?
You can access the new forum at: [forum.clientxcms.com](https://forum.clientxcms.com). There you will find sections to ask your questions, share your experiences, and exchange with other ClientXCMS users.
