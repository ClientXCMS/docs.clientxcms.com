## Nexus: A Complete Hosting Theme

Nexus goes further than a simple skin. It's a construction kit for your hosting site, available now on the [marketplace](https://clientxcms.com/resources/nexus).

- **50+ section components** to freely assemble your pages.
- **9 color palettes** to match your visual identity.
- **7 Hero banner variants** to customize your showcase.
- **Layouts designed for conversion** and clear navigation.

Here is an overview of the different pages available.

### Headache-free Configuration

No need to touch the code. Nexus is configured directly from the administration panel, section by section.

![Administration interface for configuring Nexus theme sections with visual options](/img/blog/v2.15/nexus-section-config.png)

## Improved Extension Management

The extensions page has been redesigned. You can now **select, install, and activate multiple extensions at once**, without page reloading.
