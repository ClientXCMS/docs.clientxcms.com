# Personalization

In **CLIENTXCMS**, **personalization** is the key to **creating a unique identity** and **delivering a consistent brand experience** to your customers. <br/>
The objective is strategic: **visually adapt your platform** to your brand image, **optimize the user experience** and **strengthen your company's recognition**. Successful personalization transforms a generic platform into a distinctive professional showcase that inspires trust and builds customer loyalty.

This section brings together all the tools to customize the appearance, navigation, content and optimization of your client area.

You can access personalization options from:

`Settings` > `Personalization`
